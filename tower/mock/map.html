The tower's eye is looking south, between the dotted red lines. You are the white dot in the inner ring, and the gate, marked G, is on the outer wall. Each fence can only be crossed at five places: one open gap, two locked doors and two windows.

Quiet route, solid line

56 steps through 1 locked door, crossing 0 exposed squares. Needs picks.

Loud route, dotted line

40 steps through 2 broken windows, crossing 3 exposed squares. Every break is heard.

  • Fence

    Stops you, not the gaze. Divides the rings.

  • Solid wall

    Stops you and the gaze. Casts a shadow away from the tower.

  • Hidden

    Inside the gaze but behind something solid. Safe, lit or not.

  • Exposed

    In the gaze, in the open and lit. Caught on the scan.

  • In view, dark

    The gaze reaches it, but only noise gives you away.

  • Locked door

    Needs a pick. Silent, and blocks the gaze while shut.

  • Window

    Break it to cross. Loud, and the gaze passes through.

  • Lamp

    Lights the squares around it that it can see.
