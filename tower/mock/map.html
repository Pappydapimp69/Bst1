Opticon mock map

The tower's eye is looking west, between the dotted red lines. You are the white dot in the inner ring, and the gate, marked G, is on the outer wall. Each ring is a row of cells, divided by solid walls that run out from the tower, opening onto a walkway behind bars that faces the tower: the walkway is the way round the ring and is always in view, while the cells hide you once you step into them. Each ring wall can only be crossed at five places: one open gap, two locked doors and two windows.

Quiet route, solid line

48 steps through 2 locked doors, crossing 0 exposed squares. Needs picks.

Loud route, dotted line

34 steps through 1 broken window, crossing 3 exposed squares. Every break is heard.

  • Bars

    The side of a cell that faces the tower. Stops you, not the gaze.

  • Solid wall

    Stops you and the gaze. Casts a shadow away from the tower.

  • Hidden

    Inside the gaze but behind something solid. Safe, lit or not.

  • Exposed

    In the gaze, in the open and lit. Caught on the scan.

  • In view, dark

    The gaze reaches it, but only noise gives you away.

  • Locked door

    Needs a pick. Silent, and blocks the gaze while shut.

  • Window

    Break it to cross. Loud, and the gaze passes through.

  • Lamp

    Lights the squares around it that it can see.

This map has 185 solid wall squares inside the outer wall. From the tower, 45% of the floor can be seen at some point in its sweep, and 55% of what is in its gaze right now is hidden.
